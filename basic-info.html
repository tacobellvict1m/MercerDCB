PERSONNEL SUMMARY

CONNOR REDMEN

Connor Redmen is a member of the Foundation's General Security Department, currently serving at Site-66 under the codename "Mercer."

Prior to joining the Foundation, Redmen served in the United States Marine Corps. His military experience provided him with experience in discipline, security operations, and working under high-pressure situations.

Following his recruitment into the Foundation, Redmen was assigned to General Security at Site-66.

Despite his experience, Redmen continues to struggle with the responsibilities associated with leadership and command. Internal records indicate that he places significant pressure on himself to prove that he is capable of leading others.
