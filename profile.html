PERSONNEL // OVERVIEW

PROFILE SUMMARY

Connor Redmen is a former United States Marine who eventually became a member of the SCP Foundation's General Security division at Site-66.

Connor is known for remaining relatively calm under pressure and for relying heavily on the discipline he developed during his military service.

Despite his experience, Connor continues to carry the memories of the events that led him to the Foundation.

PERSONNEL // BIOGRAPHICAL RECORD

ENTIRE LIFE STORY

Connor Redmen was born and raised in Fond du Lac, Wisconsin, alongside his mother, Carol Redmen, his father, Daren Redmen, his older brother, Carter Redmen, and his older sister, Amelia Redmen. Connor had a relatively normal and happy childhood. He was never bullied and generally got along with the people around him. In school, his grades usually ranged from a C- to a B+. Although he wasn't the smartest student in his class, he was respectful and easygoing. Connor spent most of his free time outside with friends and family. For most of his childhood, he had little reason to believe his life would ever become more complicated than that. As Connor grew older, he became more independent and started spending more time around town with his friends. He became known as someone who could stay calm when situations became heated and was usually the person trying to keep his friends from making stupid decisions. Everything changed during the summer of 2019. After a bar fight, one of Connor's closest friends, Ryan McGrie, attempted to kill him over money. What started as an argument quickly became a fight for Connor's life. Connor fought back in self-defense, but the confrontation ended with Ryan being beaten to death. Although Connor avoided serious legal consequences due to the circumstances surrounding the incident, the emotional consequences never went away. Connor couldn't stop thinking about what happened. The cheerful and trusting person he had been began disappearing, replaced by someone quieter, more cautious, and afraid to let people get close. Not long after the incident, Connor decided that he needed to get away from everything he had known. Looking for structure, purpose, and a way to rebuild himself, he enlisted in the United States Marine Corps. Marine life was difficult, but Connor adapted quickly. The discipline, physical training, and responsibility gave him something to focus on. Over time, he became a dependable and capable Marine. During one deployment, Connor's unit was ordered to investigate an abandoned research facility after reports of missing civilians in the surrounding area. They expected criminals, insurgents, or smugglers. Instead, they encountered something none of them could explain. Deep inside the abandoned structure, they encountered an unknown entity that moved almost completely silently and seemed capable of disappearing from sight. Several Marines were injured before an unidentified tactical team arrived and eliminated the threat. The operators were unlike anyone Connor had ever encountered. They wore equipment Connor couldn't identify, carried themselves with military precision, and seemed to know exactly what they were dealing with. Connor's unit was ordered to remain silent about everything they had witnessed. Connor and the surviving Marines were detained afterward and questioned for several days by people claiming to represent a government organization. Eventually, Connor was subjected to a memory-altering procedure. Most of the Marines forgot the incident entirely. Connor did not. He retained fragments of what happened — flashes of the creature, strange symbols, unidentified operators, and the feeling that something about the entire incident had been deliberately hidden from him. Unknown to Connor, the organization responsible was the SCP Foundation. Foundation personnel noticed Connor's unusual resistance to the memory treatment and quietly began monitoring him. His military record, ability to remain calm under pressure, and resistance to amnestic treatment made him someone the Foundation considered worth watching. After completing his service in the Marines, Connor returned to civilian life. He had accomplished what he had set out to do, but found himself struggling without the structure and purpose the military had provided. He still thought about the abandoned facility and the thing he had seen inside it. Sometimes he wondered if the entire experience had actually happened or if the memories he retained were simply fragments of something his mind couldn't properly understand. Then, on December 12th, 2019, Connor walked into a small local bar wearing his usual cowboy hat. He ordered a shot of whiskey and sat by himself for a while, trying to clear his head. Eventually, he stepped outside for a cigarette. As snow fell around him and Christmas lights blinked across the nearby buildings, two strangers approached him. They were Foundation recruiters. They told Connor the truth. The facility from his deployment had been real. The creature had been real. The people who arrived afterward had been Foundation personnel. They explained that the world contained things far beyond what ordinary governments and militaries knew about. They offered Connor a position within Foundation Security. Connor listened without saying much. After everything he had experienced, the revelation was almost strangely comforting. For the first time, the memories that had haunted him had an explanation. Connor threw his cigarette onto the snow and spat on it before accepting their offer. He exchanged the necessary information with the recruiters, watching as they disappeared down the snowy street beneath the Christmas lights. Connor stood there for a moment, thinking about everything that had happened since Ryan McGrie's death. He had joined the Marines looking for purpose, encountered something impossible, and now the organization responsible for that impossible world was offering him a place inside it. Before leaving for the Foundation, Connor received a letter that he kept with him. He didn't talk about the letter often, but he carried it wherever he went, keeping it close as a reminder of the life he had left behind. After completing specialized Foundation training and receiving the necessary security clearance, Connor was assigned to SCP Site-66 as a member of GENSEC. The Marines had taught him how to survive combat, follow orders, and protect the people beside him. The Foundation would teach him something entirely different. Not every enemy could be understood. Not every threat followed the laws of nature. And sometimes the things hiding in the darkness were far worse than anything Connor had already survived. Connor Redmen had spent his entire life trying to find somewhere he belonged. Eventually, he found Site-66.
